About FulcrumNews

FulcrumNews is an open-source, self-hostable alternative to Ground News. It gathers coverage of the same story from outlets across the political spectrum, groups the articles that report the same event, and shows you how balanced (or one-sided) that coverage is.

The bias bar

Each story shows a left / center / right bar based on the number of distinct outlets from each part of the spectrum covering it. Outlet lean ratings are a consensus of AllSides, Media Bias/Fact Check, and Ad Fontes Media.

Blindspots

A Blindspot is flagged when one side of the spectrum substantially under-covers a story that the other side is reporting — so a reader who only follows that side would miss it. By default a side is a blindspot when it has fewer than 2 distinct outlets or under 15% of the coverage (both tunable).

AI briefings

For multi-outlet stories, an AI model writes a neutral briefing of the shared facts and highlights where coverage diverges. Briefings are model-generated and may contain errors — always read the outlets directly, which you can do from each story page.

Sources

FulcrumNews uses only free, publicly available feeds (RSS and the Guardian Open Platform API). Outlets that don't offer a free/legal feed (e.g. Reuters, AFP) are not included. You can add, edit, classify, and verify your own outlets from the Outlets page.
